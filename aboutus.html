Certification & Practical Projects

Upon completion of any program with DegreatCode ICT LTD, students receive an official certificate of completion. More importantly, every trainee is expected to execute and complete at least one personal project during their course. This project serves as proof that you have mastered the skills and demonstrates your practical ability to execute real-world solutions.

We ensure that our trainees not only understand the theoretical concepts but can also apply them effectively in real projects, making them job-ready upon graduation.
