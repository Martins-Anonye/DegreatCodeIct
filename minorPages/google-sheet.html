Google Sheet Notes

Google Sheets is ideal for tracking training progress, managing course schedules, and organizing tutorial data. DegreatCode ICT LTD shows how to use spreadsheets for practical learning workflows.

What you will learn

  • Building training trackers, course planners, and tutorial checklists.
  • Using formulas, charts, and sheets to manage learning resources.
  • Sharing and collaborating with students and project teams.

Google Sheets helps learners and teams stay organized while working on training, apps, and software projects.
