How verification works

This page allows anyone to confirm that a student is registered with DegreatCode ICT LTD and review their program details. Use this tool to verify enrolment, program focus, and professionalism before hiring, collaborating, or inviting someone to training.

  • Enter a registered student ID and click Verify.
  • Successful verification shows the student name, program, and status.
  • Unrecognized IDs should be checked with DegreatCode ICT LTD support.
